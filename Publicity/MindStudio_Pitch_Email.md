**Subject:** Article pitch: Where AI chats become project records

MindStudio builds tools for people creating AI workflows and automations. This article is about what happens after the workflow runs - when the decisions, trade-offs, reviews, and approvals from an AI conversation need to survive beyond the chat window.

The argument is simple: AI chats are good at generating ideas but poor at preserving engineering decisions. Once AI-assisted work becomes a real software project, chat becomes the wrong system of record.

The article describes a lightweight workflow pattern that moves important decisions into durable project records, with separate roles for multiple AI systems and a human reviewer who retains final approval authority. It includes practical mechanisms such as workflow state records, adversarial review rounds, approval gates, and scope freeze checkpoints.

The workflow evolved during long-running real-world software projects involving Python, PostgreSQL, and multi-model AI collaboration. I also used the workflow itself to govern the process that produced this article pitch.
