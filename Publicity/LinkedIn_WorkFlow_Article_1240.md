WorkFlow is what I built after watching good engineering decisions dissolve
into chat.

Somebody has an idea. Somebody else has an objection. A third person adds an
important constraint. The idea gets better.

And then the reasoning disappears into a giant thread, a meeting memory, or one
poor soul's head.

That is not a system. That is a leak.

WorkFlow is my answer to that.

At heart, it is simple: one AI proposes, another AI critiques, the Human stays
in charge, and the reasoning gets written into a durable Markdown record.

Concerns get severity. Scope gets frozen. Implementation does not begin until
the gate is actually clear.

That matters because a lot of engineering trouble starts when a design is
"mostly agreed" but not actually agreed. Then code starts growing around
assumptions nobody pinned down.

WorkFlow is meant to stop that before the concrete is poured.

Not the chat. Not the vibes. Not the "I thought we said..." The record.

If it was important enough to rely on, it is important enough to write down.
