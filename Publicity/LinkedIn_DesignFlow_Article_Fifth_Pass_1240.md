DesignFlow is what happens before the real argument starts.

Sometimes you are not ready to build. You are not even ready to debate. You
are still trying to figure out what the thing is.

That stage matters more than people admit.

A lot of bad work gets done because folks rush from "I have an idea" to "let's
implement it" without giving the idea a place to breathe. Then halfway through
the build, everybody realizes they were imagining different things and we all
act surprised.

DesignFlow is my answer to that.

It is not governance. It is not an approval chain. It is just a structured
place to think through a concept before you freeze it into something heavier.

What matters is that the important parts get written down: what you are making,
who it is for, what problem it solves, what is in scope, what is not, what
options were considered, and what questions are still open.

Not just said, not just implied, not just left sitting in transient context.

To me, that is the value. Some work is not ready for WorkFlow yet. It is still
becoming itself.
